# Data sources

Every source is open or public, and we publish only our own counts and charts, not source text.

| Source | Represents | Cannot tell us | Terms and attribution | Redistribute source text? | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google Trends | Public search attention | Why people searched; sales | Use allowed under Google Terms of Service, with attribution: Data source: Google Trends (https://www.google.com/trends) | Aggregated numbers with attribution only | In use |
| Wikipedia page views (Wikimedia) | Attention to articles | Why people looked; who they are | Wikimedia terms not yet checked | No, until checked | To check, then collect |
| GDELT Project | Media framing: tone, themes, volume | What consumers think | Free for academic use with citation: https://www.gdeltproject.org/about.html#termsofuse. We avoid BigQuery, which can cost money | No. We publish our own counts only | To collect |
| BCG-Altagamma True-Luxury Global Consumer Insights 2026 | Consumer motivations (survey) | Trends over time; individual behaviour | Public report. Findings cited in our own words | No. No copied tables, PDF not hosted | To read and cite |
| Amazon Reviews 2023 (optional) | Buyer opinions up to Sep 2023 | Luxury in general; 2025-2026 | Licence not yet checked. Reviewer IDs must be dropped | No | Optional, later |

## Rules we follow
1. Credit every source.
2. Publish our own numbers and charts, never copied text.
3. Never publish personal information.
4. Check each source's terms separately before using it.
